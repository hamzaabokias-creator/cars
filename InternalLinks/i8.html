BMW i8

A PLUG-IN HYBRID SPORTS CAR WITH EXCEPTIONAL PERFORMANCE AND EFFICIENCY

BMW i8

The BMW i8 is a revolutionary plug-in hybrid sports car developed by BMW. As the flagship halo car of BMW's forward-looking i-sub brand, it seamlessly blends everyday efficiency with exotic mid-engine aesthetics, rewriting the playbook for modern alternative-performance sports coupes.

Specifications

  • Top Speed: 250 km/h (Electronically Limited)
  • Acceleration 0-100 km/h: 4.4 seconds
  • Range: Up to 550 km total (37 km pure electric range)
  • Battery Capacity: 11.6 kWh Lithium-ion Battery pack
  • Drive Type: All-Wheel Drive (Through hybrid coordination)
  • Motor Type: 1.5L Turbocharged 3-Cylinder Engine & Electric Motor
  • Charging Time: 0% to 100% in approx. 3.1 hours (on Level 2 charging)
  • Price: Approx. $147,500 new (Varies on used market)
  • Weight: 1,539 kg to 1,595 kg
  • Dimensions: Length 4,689 mm, Width 1,942 mm, Height 1,291 mm
  • Seating Capacity: 4 passengers (2+2 layout)

Features

  • Lightweight LifeDrive architecture combining a carbon-fiber reinforced plastic (CFRP) cell with an aluminum chassis
  • Striking dihedral butterfly doors that open upward and outward for an undeniable hypercar presence
  • Intelligent eDrive drivetrain dynamically managing power between the electric front axle and gas rear axle
  • Functional aero tunnels and layered body panels dropping the drag coefficient down to an ultra-slick 0.26 Cd
  • Eco-focused modern interior lines making heavy use of recycled plastics, sustainable fabrics, and olive-leaf tanned leathers
  • Adaptive driving cluster shifting instrumentation visual styles and colors based on Comfort, Eco Pro, or Sport modes

Why Choose the BMW i8?

You want cutting-edge tech: Choose it if you want an early pioneer of lightweight carbon monocoque architecture and smart split-axle power routing.You love family utility: Choose it if you need a tight pair of rear seating positions capable of stowing daily carry-ons or groceries.You want hypercar power for less: Choose it if you want head-turning concept car aesthetics and active presence on a vastly discounted secondhand budget.You value a dual-purpose cabin: Choose it if you want a machine capable of a whisper-quiet, pure electric morning commute that transforms instantly into a high-revving sports car.You have access to high-power grid charging: Choose it if you can tap into standard household or garage outlets overnight to avoid burning gas during local errands.

Why not Choose the BMW i8?

The BMW i8 falls short for pure acoustic purists who miss the roaring engine notes of rival mid-engine cars due to its synthesised exhaust and tiny 3-cylinder base. Its ultra-wide doors and high, wide carbon sills make tight parking slots a complete nightmare and complicate daily entry or exit. Furthermore, long-distance owners face significant specialty maintenance friction, as complex high-voltage battery repairs and specialized body panels necessitate trips to highly certified BMW i-dealership technicians.
