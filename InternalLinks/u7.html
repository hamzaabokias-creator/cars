YANGWANG U7

AN ELECTRIC SUPER SEDAN WITH EXCEPTIONAL PERFORMANCE AND RANGE

YANGWANG U7

The Yangwang U7 is an all-electric flagship luxury super sedan built by BYD's ultra-luxury sub-brand, Yangwang. It is designed to redefine the luxury segment by combining sports-car performance with elite limousine comfort and advanced robotic control systems.

Specifications

  • Top Speed: 270 km/h
  • Acceleration 0-100 km/h: 2.9 seconds
  • Range: 720 km to 1,006 km (CLTC)
  • Battery Capacity: 135.5 kWh to 150 kWh (Blade Battery)
  • Drive Type: All-Wheel Drive (e4 Platform)
  • Motor Type: Quad Electric Motors (4 Motors)
  • Charging Time: 30% to 80% in 20 minutes (DC Fast Charging)
  • Price: 628,000 RMB (Approx. $89,800)
  • Weight: 3,095 kg
  • Dimensions: Length 5,360 mm, Width 2,000 mm, Height 1,515 mm
  • Seating Capacity: 4 or 5 passengers

Features

  • DiSus-Z Intelligent Active Suspension utilizing localized electric motors for unparalleled body control and flat ride quality
  • Ultra-low aerodynamic drag profile with a 0.195 Cd rating for enhanced highway efficiency and stability
  • Intelligent Torque Vectoring via the e4 platform providing millisecond-level independent wheel adjustments
  • Roof-mounted LiDAR sensor suite driving high-level autonomous navigation and adaptive safety features
  • Luxury executive cockpit featuring premium leather upholstery, multiple digital displays, and deep integration across systems
  • State-of-the-art thermal management keeping the high-capacity Blade Battery 2.0 at optimal operating temperatures

Why Choose the Yangwang U7?

You want cutting-edge tech: Choose it if you want an ultra-intelligent luxury cruiser equipped with four independent electric motors and active computing suspension.You love executive comfort: Choose it if you need the high-end interior, massive passenger space, and smooth ride of a true luxury flagship.You want hypercar power for less: Choose it if you want access to over 1,300 horsepower at a fraction of the cost of European luxury performance vehicles.You value an extreme driving range: Choose it if you prioritize long highway legs and want a battery capable of breaking the 1,000 km barrier under ideal conditions.You need real everyday versatility: Choose it if you want an absolute powerhouse that gracefully switches between track-day pacing and quiet business transit.

Why not Choose the Yangwang U7?

The Yangwang U7 faces structural challenges as a pure enthusiast car due to its hefty 3.1-ton curb weight, which impacts agility despite the high power output. Its massive size and long executive footprint make navigating tight inner-city turns or packed parking structures notably cumbersome. Furthermore, standard global buyers will experience major logistical friction due to a limited international presence, leaving long-distance service networks and localized software updates thin outside primary markets.
