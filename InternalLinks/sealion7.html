BYD SEALION 7

AN ELECTRIC SUV WITH EXCEPTIONAL PERFORMANCE AND RANGE

BYD SEALION 7

The BYD Sealion 7 is a mid-size all-electric fastback SUV built on BYD's highly advanced e-Platform 3.0 Evo. It balances a sleek, aerodynamic coupe silhouette with family-focused utility, aiming to challenge established global rivals in the premium electric crossover market.

Specifications

  • Top Speed: 215 km/h
  • Acceleration 0-100 km/h: 4.5 seconds (AWD variant)
  • Range: 550 km to 610 km (CLTC)
  • Battery Capacity: 71.8 kWh to 80.6 kWh (LFP Blade Battery)
  • Drive Type: Rear-Wheel Drive / All-Wheel Drive
  • Motor Type: Single or Dual Electric Motors (up to 23,000 RPM)
  • Charging Time: 10% to 80% in 25 minutes (High-Voltage Fast Charging)
  • Price: 189,800 RMB (Approx. $26,500 base)
  • Weight: 2,140 kg to 2,330 kg
  • Dimensions: Length 4,830 mm, Width 1,925 mm, Height 1,620 mm
  • Seating Capacity: 5 passengers

Features

  • Cell-to-Body (CTB) structural architecture embedding the battery packs cleanly into the vehicle frame for high torsional rigidity
  • Ultra-high-speed electric motor reaching up to 23,000 RPM to sustain strong continuous acceleration performance
  • DiPilot advanced driver assistance suite providing level 2 highway navigation and automated parking support
  • Rotatable 15.6-inch infotainment touchscreen running the latest smart cockpit software ecosystem
  • Advanced frequency-selective damping suspension managing body lean through corners while absorbing minor road imperfections
  • High-efficiency heat pump system minimizing real-world range degradation during extreme winter conditions

Why Choose the BYD Sealion 7?

You want cutting-edge tech: Choose it if you want the structural benefits of Cell-to-Body integration and a next-generation high-RPM motor platform.You love family utility: Choose it if you need a flexible 5-seater layout with a practical hatchback trunk and an additional front storage frunk.You want hypercar power for less: Choose it if you want quick sub-5-second acceleration without paying premium European performance SUV prices.You value a dual-purpose cabin: Choose it if you want a premium interior that isolates outside highway noise while staying highly durable for messy daily tasks.You have access to high-power grid charging: Choose it if your local infrastructure supports boosted high-voltage stations to maximize its dual-gun charging speeds.

Why not Choose the BYD Sealion 7?

The BYD Sealion 7 falls short for performance purists looking for sharp handling, as its heavy curb weight and soft suspension cause noticeable body roll on winding backroads. Its aggressive sloping roofline compromises rear headroom for taller passengers and reduces bulk cargo capacity compared to boxier SUVs. Furthermore, buyers outside core global launch markets may encounter regional software limits and delayed navigation map updates due to rolling international distributions.
