BYD Sealion 7

Electric SUV with modern design and advanced technology.

The BYD Sealion 7 is a premium electric crossover from BYD featuring a sharp exterior, spacious interior, and long-range battery performance. Designed for everyday driving, it blends comfort, style, and efficiency in one package.

Overview

The BYD Sealion 7 is based on BYD's latest electric vehicle architecture and offers a competitive range, smart connectivity, and safety technology. It appeals to drivers who want a refined EV in the compact SUV segment with strong value and low running costs.

Key highlights include a fast-charging capability, a roomy cabin for five passengers, and a suite of driver assistance features.

Specifications

  • Powertrain: Electric motor
  • Battery: Long-range lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery
  • Range: Up to 600 km (WLTP estimate)*
  • Performance: Quick acceleration with smooth electric delivery
  • Seating: 5 passengers
  • Infotainment: Large touchscreen display with smartphone connectivity
  • Safety: Advanced driver assistance systems and multiple airbags

Why Choose the Sealion 7?

The Sealion 7 offers an attractive combination of modern technology, low emissions, and practical range for daily use. It is well suited for families and commuters who want a comfortable ride with the latest electric vehicle features.

Its styling, interior space, and efficient electric drivetrain make it a strong choice among compact electric SUVs.
