BMW M8

A LUXURY GT SUPER-COUPE WITH EXCEPTIONAL PERFORMANCE AND PRESENCE

BMW M8

The BMW M8 Competition is a high-performance grand touring supercar engineered by BMW's M division. Serving as the absolute flagship of the brand's luxury lineup, it blends devastating racetrack capabilities with everyday executive refinement to challenge the world's finest grand tourers.

Specifications

  • Top Speed: 305 km/h (With M Driver's Package)
  • Acceleration 0-100 km/h: 3.2 seconds
  • Range: Approx. 580 km (Gasoline highway cruising)
  • Battery Capacity: N/A (Traditional internal combustion engine)
  • Drive Type: M xDrive All-Wheel Drive (with 2WD pure rear-wheel mode)
  • Motor Type: 4.4L Twin-Turbocharged V8 Gas Engine
  • Charging Time: N/A (Standard premium unleaded refueling)
  • Price: Approx. $138,800 base MSRP
  • Weight: 1,975 kg to 2,030 kg
  • Dimensions: Length 4,867 mm, Width 1,907 mm, Height 1,362 mm
  • Seating Capacity: 4 passengers (2+2 seating setup)

Features

  • High-revving M TwinPower Turbo V8 motor delivering 617 horsepower and an aggressive exhaust note
  • Configurable M xDrive powertrain capable of shifting fully to 2WD rear-wheel drive for pure drift track days
  • Integrated carbon-fiber reinforced plastic (CFRP) structural core minimizing weight along the roofline and pillars
  • Adaptive M Suspension with electronically controlled dampers that recalculate road physics millisecond by millisecond
  • Luxury digital driver cockpit featuring visual M-mode heads-up displays and premium Merino leather stitching
  • Advanced adjustable braking setup allowing drivers to customize brake pedal feel across comfort and sport profiles

Why Choose the BMW M8?

You want cutting-edge tech: Choose it if you want highly customizable performance tracking, a decoupled variable brake system, and an smart switchable AWD drivetrain.You love family utility: Choose it if you need a spacious grand tourer with a substantial 420-liter trunk capacity ready for cross-country luggage.You want hypercar power for less: Choose it if you want relentless sub-3.5-second acceleration capabilities that rival ultra-expensive exotic vehicles.You value a dual-purpose cabin: Choose it if you want an ultra-quiet luxury sanctuary that transitions into a loud, hard-cornering track machine at the tap of an M button.You have access to high-power grid charging: Choose it if you prefer avoiding charging logistics entirely and want quick, traditional pump refills to get back on the road in minutes.

Why not Choose the BMW M8?

The BMW M8 falls short for green-focused commuters due to its heavy, thirsty twin-turbo V8 engine and high daily emissions footprints. Its sloped luxury profile and high interior dash lines make rear-seat legroom incredibly cramped for adults on extended road trips. Furthermore, owners looking for pure analog simplicity will feel overwhelmed by the endless digital menu settings, complex electronic driver profiles, and heavy reliance on active safety computing.
