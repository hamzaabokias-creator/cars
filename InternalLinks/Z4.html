BMW Z4 M40i

The BMW Z4 M40i is a high-performance luxury soft-top roadster engineered by BMW. Built as a purist's open-top sports car, it pairs traditional rear-wheel-drive dynamics and short overhangs with a legendary turbocharged powerplant to offer an immersive, wind-in-the-hair driving experience.

  • Acceleration 0-100 km/h: 4.2 to 4.5 seconds
  • Range: Approx. 630 km (Gasoline highway cruising)
  • Battery Capacity: N/A (Traditional internal combustion engine)
  • Drive Type: Rear-Wheel Drive (RWD)
  • Motor Type: 3.0L TwinPower Turbo Inline 6-Cylinder Gas Engine (B58)
  • Charging Time: N/A (Standard premium unleaded refueling)

Features

  • Acclaimed B58 3.0-liter straight-six engine generating up to 382 horsepower with distinct exhaust pops and burbles
  • Available 6-speed manual transmission configuration via the Handschalter Package for a highly tactile, analog engagement
  • Fully electronic M Sport differential maximizing cornering traction and power delivery to individual rear wheels
  • Adaptive M Suspension with variable electronic damping that shifts seamlessly from cruise comfort to rigid track stiffness
  • Lightweight electric insulated canvas soft-top operating in under 10 seconds at driving speeds up to 50 km/h
  • Classic layout boasting an optimal 50:50 front-to-rear vehicle weight distribution for ultra-neutral handling dynamics

Why Choose the BMW Z4 M40i?

You want cutting-edge tech: Choose it if you want an open-top cruiser with high-fidelity digital cockpit professional readouts and advanced variable sport steering setups.You love family utility: Choose it if you need a tight 281-liter deep trunk shape capable of cleanly stowing away two standard hard-case carry-on travel bags.You want hypercar power for less: Choose it if you want sub-4.5-second straight-line acceleration and pure sports car dynamics without breaking six-figure vehicle budgets.You value a dual-purpose cabin: Choose it if you want an ultra-refined, isolated highway tourer that drops its roof instantly to tackle aggressive mountain switchbacks.You have access to high-power grid charging: Choose it if you prefer avoiding public charging wait times completely and value rapid fuel pump fill-ups on cross-country road trips.

Why not Choose the BMW Z4 M40i?

The BMW Z4 M40i falls short for carpooling duties due to its strict two-seat cabin limit and utter lack of emergency rear passenger space. Its classic low-slung front nose profile and low ride height require extreme caution to avoid costly scrapes on steep driveways or concrete city parking blocks. Furthermore, long-term tech collectors should take note that this vehicle line concludes production globally in early 2026, which may squeeze future local parts sourcing pipelines and mechanical repairs down the road.
