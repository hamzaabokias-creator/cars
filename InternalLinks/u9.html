YANGWANG U9

AN ELECTRIC SUPERCAR WITH EXCEPTIONAL PERFORMANCE AND RANGE

YANGWANG U9

The Yangwang U9 is an all-electric supercar built by BYD's ultra-luxury sub-brand, Yangwang. It is designed to compete directly with traditional European supercars by combining massive electric power with advanced robotic suspension technology.

Specifications

  • Top Speed: 309.19 km/h
  • Acceleration 0-100 km/h: 2.36 seconds
  • Range: 450 km (CLTC)
  • Battery Capacity: 80 kWh
  • Drive Type: All-Wheel Drive (e4 Platform)
  • Motor Type: Quad Electric Motors (4 Motors)
  • Charging Time: 30% to 80% in 10 minutes (DC Dual Fast Charging)
  • Price: 1,680,000 RMB (Approx. $236,000)
  • Weight: 2,475 kg
  • Dimensions: Length 4,966 mm, Width 2,029 mm, Height 1,295 mm
  • Seating Capacity: 2 passengers

Features

  • DiSus-X Intelligent Active Suspension capable of hopping, dancing, and driving on three wheels
  • Advanced Carbon Fiber Monocoque cockpit structure for maximum rigidity and safety
  • Intelligent Torque Vectoring providing independent wheel control and instant traction management
  • Active Aerodynamics package with a deployable electric rear wing and thermal management air ducts
  • Luxury dual-screen cockpit utilizing high-quality materials and tailored racing seats
  • Regenerative braking integration combined with carbon-ceramic high-performance brakes

Why Choose the Yangwang U9?

You want cutting-edge tech: Choose it if you want the world's most advanced robotic suspension and independent quad-motor torque vectoring.You love track-day innovations: Choose it if you want an EV designed with extensive thermal cooling and carbon-ceramic brakes for sustained track use.You want hypercar power for less: Choose it if you want 1,288 horsepower without paying the $2M+ price tag of a Rimac or Pininfarina.You value a dual-purpose cabin: Choose it if you want a car that switches from a stiff track weapon to a quiet, comfortable daily driver.You have access to high-power grid charging: Choose it if you can regularly utilize dual-plug DC fast chargers to get back on the road in 10 minutes.

Why not Choose the Yangwang U9?

the Yangwang U9 fails to deliver the raw, visceral thrill of a traditional combustion-engine supercar due to its silent electric powertrain and immense 2.5-ton curb weight. Its limited real-world range and rapid battery depletion under aggressive driving heavily compromise its track-day longevity. Furthermore, a highly restricted global dealer network makes local servicing, parts availability, and long-term maintenance an logistical nightmare for international buyers.
